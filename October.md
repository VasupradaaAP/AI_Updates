## MAIN TOPIC - 08/10/2026

* Maintaining core technical understanding while using AI coding tools, highlighting advice from Zoho co-founder Sridhar Vembu.

## Complete Summary

**Context & Sridhar Vembu's Warning:**
* A software engineer expressed frustration on X (formerly Twitter) about developers relying entirely on AI (like Claude Code) to generate code, tests, and documentation without reviewing or understanding the underlying logic due to pressure to ship faster.
* Zoho Co-Founder Sridhar Vembu reacted to this post, stating: *"Tell our engineers to use AI, but never cede our understanding to AI."*


**The Core Analogy:**
* Blindly relying on AI code generation is like a student copying answers directly from an answer key—they may get the right answer on paper, but they will fail in an interview or exam when asked to explain the fundamental method.
* AI-generated code may work initially, but if it breaks later, developers who do not understand the underlying implementation will be unable to debug or fix it.


**Sequential Steps to Safely Evaluate AI-Generated Code:**
1. Ask: **How does it work?** (Understand the core logic generated by the tool).
2. Ask: **Where could it fail?** (Identify edge cases, limitations, or failure modes).
3. Ask: **Can I explain this to someone else?** (Ensure you can articulate the implementation clearly).
4. **Verify and test:** Fact-check and test the explanations and code generated by the AI model rather than blindly accepting them.

## Key Takeaways

* **Accelerate work, not complete reliance:** AI should be leveraged to boost speed and productivity, but domain comprehension and technical mastery must remain with the developer.
* **Critical evaluation standard:** Before approving or merging AI-generated solutions, always evaluate how it works, its failure points, and whether you can independently explain and maintain the solution.
